<Accordion title="What is Elliott Strategies?">
  Elliott Strategies is a strategic advisory and platform solution designed to help organizations plan, execute, and track their business strategies with clarity and confidence. The platform gives leadership teams a centralized space to define goals, build strategic plans, assign ownership, track metrics, and measure progress — all in one place.

  Unlike project management tools that focus on task execution, Elliott Strategies is built around the strategic layer: connecting high-level objectives to the initiatives and metrics that show whether you're actually moving in the right direction.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I invite my team?">
  To invite teammates to your workspace:

  1. Go to **Settings → Team Members**.
  2. Click **Invite Member**.
  3. Enter the email addresses of the people you want to invite (you can enter multiple addresses at once, separated by commas).
  4. Select a role for each invitee — **Admin**, **Contributor**, or **Member**.
  5. Click **Send Invitations**.

  Each invitee receives an email with a link to join your workspace. Invitations expire after 7 days — if a teammate doesn't receive theirs, check their spam folder or resend the invitation from the Team Members page.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What are the different user roles?">
  Elliott Strategies uses three roles to control access within a workspace:

  - **Workspace Owner** — Full control over the workspace, including billing, settings, and the ability to delete the workspace. There can only be one Owner per workspace, but ownership can be transferred.
  - **Admin** — Can manage team members, configure integrations, and edit all strategies and settings. Ideal for team leads and operations managers.
  - **Contributor** — Can create and edit strategies, initiatives, and metrics they own. Cannot access workspace-level settings or manage other users.
  - **Member** — Read-only access. Can view published strategies and leave comments, but cannot create or edit content.

  Admins and the Workspace Owner can change any member's role at any time from **Settings → Team Members**.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I have multiple workspaces?">
  Yes. Your Elliott Strategies account can belong to multiple workspaces — for example, if you consult for several organizations or manage distinct business units that need separate environments. Each workspace has its own strategies, team members, integrations, and billing.

  To create a new workspace, click your workspace name in the top-left corner of the sidebar and select **Create New Workspace**. To switch between workspaces you belong to, use the same menu.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I export a strategy report?">
  You can export any strategy as a polished PDF or as a CSV data file:

  1. Open the strategy you want to export.
  2. Click the **•••** (More Options) menu in the top-right corner of the strategy view.
  3. Select **Export**.
  4. Choose your format: **PDF Report** (formatted for sharing with stakeholders) or **CSV Data** (raw data for further analysis).
  5. Click **Download**. The file saves to your browser's default download location.

  PDF reports include your workspace logo, strategy name, objectives, initiatives, metrics, and progress summaries as of the export date.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I connect third-party tools?">
  Elliott Strategies integrates with tools like Slack, Google Sheets, Jira, and Microsoft Teams. To set up an integration:

  1. Go to **Settings → Integrations**.
  2. Find the tool you want to connect and click **Connect**.
  3. Authenticate with your credentials for that service.
  4. Configure the sync or notification settings.
  5. Click **Save**.

  Setting up integrations requires the **Admin** role. For a full walkthrough of each integration, see the [Integrations guide](/configuration/integrations).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens when a strategy is archived?">
  Archiving a strategy removes it from your active workspace view without permanently deleting it. Archived strategies:

  - Are no longer visible in your main strategy dashboard.
  - Remain fully accessible under **Strategies → Archived** for reference or audit purposes.
  - Can be restored to active status at any time by an Admin or Workspace Owner.
  - Retain all their associated initiatives, metrics, and historical data.

  Archiving is the recommended approach when a strategy cycle ends — it keeps your workspace clean while preserving your organization's institutional memory.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How is my data secured?">
  Elliott Strategies takes data security seriously. Key protections include:

  - **Encryption in transit and at rest** — All data is encrypted using TLS 1.2+ in transit and AES-256 at rest.
  - **Role-based access control** — Workspace roles ensure members can only see and edit what they're permitted to.
  - **Two-factor authentication** — Available to all users and enforceable at the workspace level by Admins.
  - **SOC 2 Type II compliance** — Elliott Strategies undergoes annual third-party audits to verify our security controls.
  - **Regular backups** — Workspace data is backed up daily, with point-in-time recovery available.

  For detailed information, review our [Privacy Policy](https://elliottstrategies.com/privacy) and [Security Overview](https://elliottstrategies.com/security).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I import data from another tool?">
  Yes. Elliott Strategies supports CSV import for strategies, initiatives, and metrics. To import:

  1. Go to **Settings → Import**.
  2. Download the relevant CSV template for the type of data you want to import.
  3. Fill in your data following the template structure.
  4. Upload the completed file and click **Preview Import**.
  5. Review the mapped fields, correct any errors flagged by the validator, and click **Confirm Import**.

  For teams migrating from spreadsheet-based planning tools, the Google Sheets integration is often a more efficient ongoing solution than a one-time CSV import.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I cancel or change my subscription?">
  To manage your subscription:

  1. Go to **Settings → Billing**.
  2. To upgrade or downgrade, click **Change Plan** and select your new plan. Changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.
  3. To cancel, click **Cancel Subscription** and follow the confirmation steps. Your workspace remains active until the end of your current paid period — you won't be charged again after cancellation.

  If you need help choosing a plan or have questions about enterprise pricing, email **support@elliottstrategies.com** or visit our [Pricing page](https://elliottstrategies.com/pricing).
</Accordion>

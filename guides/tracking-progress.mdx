Keeping your strategy current is just as important as building it. Elliott Strategies gives you a structured workflow for updating initiative progress, marking milestones complete, and recording metric values — so your team always has an accurate, up-to-date picture of where things stand. Regular updates also ensure that your dashboard and reports reflect reality, not stale data.

## Update Initiative Progress

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the relevant Strategy">
    From the left-hand navigation, click **Strategies** and select the strategy you want to update. This opens the strategy detail view, which lists all associated initiatives.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select an Initiative">
    Click on the initiative you want to update. The initiative panel will expand to show its full details, including status, milestones, assigned metrics, and recent activity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update the initiative status">
    Click the **Status** field and choose the status that best reflects current progress: **On Track**, **At Risk**, **Behind**, or **Complete**. Add an optional progress note to give teammates context on the change.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark milestones as complete">
    Scroll to the **Milestones** section within the initiative. Click the checkbox next to each milestone that has been reached. Completed milestones are automatically timestamped and logged in the activity feed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update metric values">
    In the **Metrics** section, enter the latest values for any linked metrics. Elliott Strategies calculates progress toward target automatically and updates the sparklines on your strategy dashboard in real time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Dashboard View

The Strategy Dashboard gives you a high-level view of execution health across all initiatives in a single strategy. Open the dashboard by clicking the **Dashboard** tab at the top of any strategy detail view.

- **Progress rings** display the percentage of milestones completed per initiative, giving you an instant read on overall momentum.
- **Milestone timelines** show upcoming and overdue milestones on a visual timeline, making it easy to spot blockers before they become critical.
- **Metric sparklines** render a compact chart for each linked metric, illustrating how values have trended over time relative to the target.

Use the dashboard during team check-ins or leadership reviews to quickly surface what's on track and where attention is needed.

## Notifications

You can configure alerts to be notified automatically when initiatives fall behind schedule or metrics miss their targets. Set up and manage your notification preferences at [Notification Settings](/configuration/notifications). Alerts can be delivered in-app, by email, or both — and can be scoped to specific strategies, initiatives, or metric thresholds.

<Tip>
  Enable the **weekly digest email** in your notification settings to receive a curated summary of progress updates, upcoming milestones, and metric changes every week. It's an efficient way to stay informed without checking the platform daily.
</Tip>

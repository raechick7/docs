Strategy execution is a team sport. Elliott Strategies gives you the tools to bring the right people into your strategies, clarify who owns what, and maintain an open channel for discussion and feedback — all in the same place where the work lives. Whether you're coordinating across departments or keeping a small team in sync, the collaboration features are designed to reduce friction and increase accountability.

## Inviting Team Members

Add colleagues to Elliott Strategies so they can contribute to strategies, track progress, or stay informed as stakeholders.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Settings">
    Click the **Settings** icon at the bottom of the left-hand navigation panel to open your organization's settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the Team tab">
    Inside Settings, click the **Team** tab. This page shows all current members of your organization, their roles, and their invitation status.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Invite">
    Click the **Invite Member** button in the top-right corner of the Team page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the invitee's email address">
    Type the email address of the person you want to invite. You can enter multiple addresses at once by separating them with commas.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign a role">
    Select a role for each invitee: **Admin**, **Editor**, or **Viewer**. Roles control what each person can see and do within Elliott Strategies. You can change roles at any time from the Team settings page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Send">
    Click **Send Invitation**. Each invitee will receive an email with a link to accept the invitation and set up their account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Assigning Owners

Every strategy and initiative should have a clearly designated owner — the person accountable for driving progress and keeping information current.

To assign an owner to a **strategy**, open the strategy detail view and click the **Owner** field in the strategy header. Search for a team member by name or email and select them.

To assign an owner to an **initiative**, open the initiative panel within a strategy and click the **Owner** field. The same search-and-select workflow applies. Initiative owners receive notifications when milestones are approaching or when the initiative falls behind.

<Note>
  Users with the **Viewer** role can see strategies and initiatives they've been given access to, but cannot edit content, change statuses, or reassign ownership. If a Viewer needs to contribute, update their role to **Editor** from the Team settings page.
</Note>

## Comments and Mentions

Use comments to discuss strategy decisions, flag blockers, or share context directly on the relevant strategy or initiative — keeping conversations tied to the work instead of scattered across email or chat.

To leave a comment, open a strategy or initiative and scroll to the **Comments** section at the bottom of the panel. Type your message and click **Post**. 

To notify a specific teammate, type **@** followed by their name anywhere in a comment. They will receive an in-app notification and, depending on their settings, an email alert. Use @mentions to request input, assign follow-up actions, or draw attention to time-sensitive updates.

## Activity Feed

The **Activity Feed** provides a running log of everything that has happened within a strategy. Access it by clicking the **Activity** tab on any strategy detail view.

The feed records:

- Status changes on strategies and initiatives
- Milestone completions
- Metric value updates
- New comments and @mentions
- Ownership changes and new member additions

The activity feed is visible to all team members with access to the strategy, giving everyone a shared record of how the work has evolved over time.

<Tip>
  Establish a **weekly check-in rhythm** where each initiative owner posts a brief comment with their latest progress update. This keeps the activity feed meaningful, reduces the need for status meetings, and ensures that the data in your reports is always current.
</Tip>

Building a strategy in Elliott Strategies gives your team a shared foundation for planning and execution. From naming your strategy and defining its time horizon to assembling your team and adding initiatives, the setup process is designed to be fast and intentional — so you can move from planning to action without friction.

## Create Your Strategy

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Strategies in the sidebar">
    In the left-hand navigation panel, click **Strategies**. This opens the Strategies overview, where all of your organization's active, draft, and archived strategies are listed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click New Strategy">
    Select the **New Strategy** button in the top-right corner of the Strategies page. A setup panel will open on the right side of your screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the strategy name, description, and time horizon">
    Enter a clear, specific **Strategy Name** that communicates the goal at a glance. Add a **Description** to explain the purpose and scope. Then set the **Time Horizon** by choosing start and end dates that reflect the planning period for this strategy.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the strategy owner and team members">
    Assign a **Strategy Owner** — the person accountable for overall progress. Then add **Team Members** who will contribute to or be informed by this strategy. You can search by name or email address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the initial status to Draft">
    Choose **Draft** as the initial status. This keeps the strategy in a working state while you continue configuring it, and prevents it from surfacing prematurely to broader audiences.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the strategy">
    Click **Save Strategy**. Your new strategy is now created and visible to Editors and Admins. You can return to it at any time to make updates before publishing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Adding Initiatives

Initiatives are the concrete workstreams that live inside a strategy. Each initiative represents a defined body of work with its own owner, milestones, and metrics. Add initiatives immediately after creating your strategy to start building out the execution layer.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the strategy">
    From the Strategies list, click the name of the strategy you just created to open its detail view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add Initiative">
    Inside the strategy detail view, click the **Add Initiative** button. An initiative creation form will appear.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the initiative details">
    Enter the initiative **Name**, a short **Description**, the responsible **Owner**, and any relevant **Due Dates** or **Milestones**. You can also tag the initiative with a priority level to signal urgency.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the initiative">
    Click **Save Initiative**. The initiative will appear nested under your strategy and is immediately available for progress tracking.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Link measurable metrics to your strategy and individual initiatives from the start. Metrics give your team a real-time signal of whether the work is producing the intended outcomes — and they power the progress rings and sparklines on your strategy dashboard.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Strategies in **Draft** status are not visible to users with the Viewer role. Publish your strategy when it's ready for broader visibility by changing the status to **Active** from the strategy detail view.
</Note>

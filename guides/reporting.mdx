Elliott Strategies gives you flexible reporting tools to communicate strategy progress to any audience — whether that's a board deck, a detailed operational review, or a data export for further analysis. You can generate reports on demand, schedule them for automatic delivery, and share live report links with stakeholders who don't need a full platform account.

## Strategy Summary Report

The **Strategy Summary Report** gives you a concise, shareable snapshot of a strategy's health. It includes the strategy name and description, current status, overall progress percentage, a list of initiatives with their individual statuses, milestone completion rates, and the latest values for all linked metrics.

Generate a Summary Report any time you need a quick briefing document — for a leadership sync, a stakeholder update, or your own record-keeping. For deeper detail, use the Detailed or Metrics-Only report types described below.

## Export a Report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a Strategy">
    From the **Strategies** list, click the strategy you want to report on. This opens the strategy detail view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the export icon or go to the Reports menu">
    In the top-right corner of the strategy detail view, click the **Export** icon (a downward arrow). Alternatively, click **Reports** in the strategy's tab bar to access the full reporting menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a report type">
    Choose one of the three report types based on your needs:
    - **Summary** — a high-level overview of strategy status and progress
    - **Detailed** — a full breakdown of all initiatives, milestones, owners, and metric history
    - **Metrics-Only** — a focused export of all metric values and trend data
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a format">
    Select your preferred output format: **PDF**, **CSV**, or **PowerPoint**. The available formats may vary by report type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Export and download">
    Click the **Export** button. Elliott Strategies will generate the report and prompt you to download the file. Large reports may take a few seconds to prepare.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Scheduled Reports

Set up automated reports to be delivered by email on a weekly or monthly basis — so key stakeholders stay informed without anyone having to remember to run a report manually.

To schedule a report, open the **Reports** menu from a strategy detail view and click **Schedule Report**. Choose the report type, output format, delivery frequency (weekly or monthly), and the email addresses to send it to. Click **Save Schedule** to activate it. You can view, edit, or delete all scheduled reports from **Settings → Reports**.

## Report Formats

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="PDF">
    The PDF export is formatted for presentation and sharing. It includes the strategy header, a visual progress summary with status indicators, an initiative table with milestone and metric highlights, and a timestamp showing when the report was generated. PDF is the best choice for leadership decks, board updates, and archival records.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="CSV">
    The CSV export delivers raw, structured data suitable for analysis in Excel, Google Sheets, or any business intelligence tool. It includes one row per initiative or metric data point, depending on the report type selected. CSV is ideal for teams that need to perform their own calculations, build custom charts, or import data into external systems.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="PowerPoint">
    The PowerPoint export produces a slide deck with one slide per initiative, including status badges, milestone progress bars, and metric sparklines. Slides use your organization's default theme when available. PowerPoint is the best choice when you need an editable deck to customize before presenting to executives or external audiences.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Note>
  Only users with the **Admin** or **Editor** role can generate and export reports. **Viewers** can see live strategy data on the platform but do not have access to the export or scheduling features.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Use the **Share Live Report** option in the Reports menu to generate a shareable link that displays a read-only, auto-updating view of the strategy summary. This is perfect for stakeholders who need ongoing visibility but don't require a full Elliott Strategies account.
</Tip>

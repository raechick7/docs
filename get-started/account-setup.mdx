Setting up your Elliott Strategies account takes only a few minutes and unlocks full access to the platform's planning, execution, and tracking tools. This guide walks you through every step — from the sign-up form to a fully configured workspace — so you and your team can start building strategies right away.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Visit elliottstrategies.com and Click Sign Up">
    Open your browser and go to [elliottstrategies.com](https://elliottstrategies.com). On the homepage, click the **Sign Up** button in the top-right corner. This takes you to the account creation form where you'll enter your basic details and create your login credentials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Your Details and Create a Password">
    Fill in your first and last name, your work email address, and a secure password. Use your work email rather than a personal address — this ensures your account stays connected to your organization and makes team discovery easier when colleagues sign up. Your password must be at least twelve characters and include a mix of letters, numbers, and symbols. Click **Create Account** when you're ready.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify Your Email Address">
    Check your inbox for a verification email from Elliott Strategies. Open the email and click **Confirm My Email**. This step activates your account and is required before you can access the platform. If the email doesn't arrive within a few minutes, check your spam folder or click **Resend Verification Email** on the confirmation screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create or Join a Workspace">
    After verifying your email, you'll be directed to the workspace setup screen. A **workspace** is your organization's shared environment — it's where all strategies, initiatives, milestones, and team members live. You have two options:

    - **Create a new workspace** — Enter your organization's name and choose a unique workspace URL (for example, `acme` becomes `app.elliottstrategies.com/acme`). Click **Create Workspace** to continue.
    - **Join an existing workspace** — If your organization already uses Elliott Strategies, select **Join a Workspace** and enter the invite code or follow the invite link sent by your administrator.

    Each workspace operates independently, with its own member roster, strategies, and settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Your Profile">
    Once inside your workspace, click your avatar in the bottom-left corner and select **Edit Profile**. Add a profile photo, confirm your job title, and set your time zone so that milestone due dates and notifications are accurate for your location. A complete profile also helps your teammates identify and collaborate with you more easily across the workspace. Click **Save Changes** when you're done.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Workspace Roles

Every workspace member is assigned a role that controls what they can see and do. Assign roles carefully to keep your workspace organized and secure.

| Role | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Owner** | Full administrative control over the workspace, including billing, integrations, and the ability to delete the workspace. There is one Owner per workspace. |
| **Admin** | Can manage members, configure workspace settings, and create or archive any strategy or initiative. Admins cannot access billing settings. |
| **Editor** | Can create and edit strategies, initiatives, and milestones, and update progress across all assigned work. Cannot modify workspace settings or manage members. |
| **Viewer** | Read-only access to all strategies and initiatives in the workspace. Viewers can leave comments but cannot create or edit content. |

<Note>
  If your organization uses Single Sign-On (SSO), your IT administrator can configure Elliott Strategies to authenticate through your identity provider — such as Okta, Azure AD, or Google Workspace. Contact your account representative or visit the **Security** section of your workspace settings to enable enterprise SSO login.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Invite your core team as soon as your workspace is ready. Go to **Settings → Team Members**, enter their work email addresses, and assign the appropriate roles. Early invitations mean everyone can review and contribute to strategies from day one rather than playing catch-up later.
</Tip>

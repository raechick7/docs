Elliott Strategies keeps you informed about the moments that matter most in your strategy — from approaching deadlines to metrics that have fallen below target. Because every team member has a different role and a different tolerance for interruption, you can fully customize which events notify you, whether those notifications arrive by email or in-app, and how frequently summary digests are sent. Getting your notification settings right means you stay in the loop without inbox overload.

---

## Notification Types

The table below lists every event that can trigger a notification in Elliott Strategies, along with a description and its default state for new accounts.

| Notification Event | Description | Default |
|---|---|---|
| Milestone due in 7 days | Sent when a milestone you own or follow is seven days from its due date | **On** |
| Initiative status changed | Triggered when an initiative's status moves to On Track, At Risk, or Off Track | **On** |
| Metric below target | Fires when a tracked metric drops below its defined target threshold | **On** |
| New comment or mention | Sent when someone comments on a strategy item you own or explicitly mentions you with `@` | **On** |
| Weekly digest email | A summary of your workspace's strategy activity sent every Monday morning | **On** |
| Strategy published | Triggered when a strategy document is published or shared with you | **On** |
| Team member invited | Sent when a new member is added to your workspace | **Off** |

## Managing Your Notification Preferences

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Click your avatar or initials in the bottom-left corner of the sidebar, then select **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Notifications">
    Click **Notifications** in the left-hand settings navigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Toggle individual notification events">
    Each event listed in the table above appears with an on/off toggle. Enable or disable each one according to your preference. Changes take effect immediately — you don't need to save individually.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set digest frequency">
    In the **Email Digest** section, use the dropdown to choose how often you want the summary email: **Weekly** (default), **Daily**, or **Never**. If you choose Daily, you can also select a preferred delivery time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your preferences">
    Click **Save Notification Settings** at the bottom of the page. A confirmation message confirms your preferences have been updated.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Email vs. In-App Notifications

Elliott Strategies delivers notifications through two distinct channels, and you can configure each one independently.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Email Notifications">
    Email notifications are sent to the address associated with your Elliott Strategies account. They're ideal for asynchronous updates — events you want a record of but don't need to act on immediately. The weekly digest is always email-only. To change your notification email address, update it in **Profile Settings**.

    Use the **Email** column toggles on the Notifications settings page to control which events generate an email. You can disable email for high-frequency events (like comments) while keeping them active in-app.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="In-App Notifications">
    In-app notifications appear in the **Notifications** bell icon in the top-right corner of Elliott Strategies. A red badge shows your unread count. Clicking the bell opens a panel with a chronological feed of all recent events.

    In-app notifications are best for real-time awareness while you're actively working in the platform. You can mark individual notifications as read, or click **Mark All as Read** to clear the panel at once. Notifications are stored in your feed for 90 days.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Tip>
  For real-time alerts on critical events like metrics falling below target or milestone deadlines, connect the **Slack integration** and route those specific notifications to a dedicated strategy channel. This gives your leadership team instant visibility without relying on anyone checking their email.
</Tip>

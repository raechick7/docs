Elliott Strategies gives you fine-grained control over how your workspace looks and behaves, how your profile appears to teammates, and how your account stays secure. Whether you're setting a company logo, updating your email address, or enabling two-factor authentication, all of these options live in one central place — your **Settings** panel, accessible from the sidebar at any time.

---

## Workspace Settings

Workspace-level settings control the identity and defaults shared across your entire organization in Elliott Strategies. You can set your workspace name, upload a logo, choose a default timezone, and select the default language for all members.

<Note>
  Only users with the **Workspace Owner** role can modify workspace-level settings. If you don't see the options below, contact your Workspace Owner to request a role change.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Click your workspace name in the top-left corner of the sidebar, then select **Settings** from the dropdown menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to Workspace">
    In the Settings panel, select **Workspace** from the left-hand navigation list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update your workspace name">
    In the **Workspace Name** field, type the name you want displayed across the platform and in all exported reports.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload a logo">
    Click **Upload Logo**, select a PNG or SVG file (recommended size: 200×200 px), and confirm the upload. Your logo appears in the sidebar and on published strategy documents.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set timezone and language">
    Use the **Timezone** dropdown to select your organization's primary timezone. Use the **Default Language** dropdown to set the display language for all workspace members. Individual members can override the language in their own Profile Settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save changes">
    Click **Save Workspace Settings**. A confirmation banner appears at the top of the screen when the update is successful.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Set the correct timezone before you start creating milestones and deadlines. Elliott Strategies calculates all due-date alerts and digest schedules relative to the workspace timezone — an incorrect setting can cause teammates in different regions to miss critical deadline notifications.
</Tip>

## Profile Settings

Profile settings are personal — they affect only your own account and how you appear to other members of your workspace.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your profile">
    Click your avatar or initials in the bottom-left corner of the sidebar, then select **Profile Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update your name">
    Edit the **First Name** and **Last Name** fields, then click **Save Profile**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change your email address">
    Enter a new address in the **Email** field and click **Update Email**. Elliott Strategies sends a verification link to your new address — you must confirm it before the change takes effect.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload a profile avatar">
    Click the camera icon overlaying your current avatar, choose an image file (JPG or PNG, max 5 MB), crop as needed, and click **Save**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change your password">
    Scroll to the **Password** section, enter your current password, type your new password twice to confirm, and click **Update Password**. Passwords must be at least 12 characters and include a mix of letters, numbers, and symbols.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Security Settings

Keep your account protected with two-factor authentication, active session oversight, and management of connected third-party apps.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable two-factor authentication (2FA)">
    Go to **Settings → Security** and click **Enable Two-Factor Authentication**. Scan the QR code with an authenticator app (such as Google Authenticator or Authy), enter the six-digit code to confirm, and save your backup codes in a secure location.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review active sessions">
    Under **Active Sessions**, you'll see every device and browser currently logged into your account, along with the last-seen timestamp and approximate location. Click **Revoke** next to any session you don't recognize to immediately sign that device out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manage connected apps">
    Under **Connected Applications**, review all third-party integrations that have been granted access to your account. Click **Disconnect** next to any app you no longer use or trust. Disconnecting an app revokes its access token immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  If you see an active session or connected app you don't recognize, revoke it immediately and change your password. Then contact **support@elliottstrategies.com** to report the suspicious activity.
</Warning>

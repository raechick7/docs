This quickstart walks you through everything you need to go from a brand-new account to a live, collaborative strategy workspace. By the end, you'll have a workspace configured, your first strategy defined with initiatives and milestones, and your teammates ready to contribute — all in under fifteen minutes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign Up for an Account">
    Navigate to [elliottstrategies.com](https://elliottstrategies.com) and click **Get Started**. Enter your name, work email address, and a secure password, then click **Create Account**. You'll receive a verification email — open it and click the confirmation link to activate your account before proceeding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create Your First Workspace">
    After verifying your email, you'll be prompted to set up a workspace. A workspace is your organization's private, shared environment where all strategies, initiatives, and team activity live. Enter your organization's name, choose a workspace URL slug, and click **Create Workspace**. If your organization already has a workspace, select **Join an Existing Workspace** and enter the invite code provided by your administrator.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Define Your First Strategy">
    From the workspace dashboard, click **New Strategy** in the left sidebar. Give your strategy a clear, outcome-focused name (for example, "Expand into EMEA Markets — FY2025") and add a brief description explaining its purpose. Set a start date and target completion date, then assign a primary owner who will be accountable for overall progress. Click **Save Strategy** to continue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Initiatives and Milestones">
    Open your new strategy and click **Add Initiative** to break the strategy into actionable workstreams. For each initiative, provide a name, an owner, and a target date. Then, within each initiative, click **Add Milestone** to define the discrete, measurable checkpoints that mark meaningful progress. Milestones give your team clear targets to hit and make it easy to spot delays early.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite Your Team">
    Click **Settings** in the left sidebar, then select **Team Members**. Enter your teammates' email addresses and assign each person an appropriate role — Admin, Editor, or Viewer — based on the level of access they need. Click **Send Invites**. Your teammates will receive an email with a link to join the workspace and get started immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Once your workspace is set up, spend a few minutes exploring the **Dashboard**. It surfaces a real-time overview of all active strategies, upcoming milestones, and initiative health scores — giving you and your team an at-a-glance view of where things stand at any moment.
</Tip>

## How It Works

Elliott Strategies is designed to get your organization aligned quickly. Follow these three steps to go from zero to a fully operational strategy in your workspace.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Your Account">
    Sign up at elliottstrategies.com, verify your email, and either create a new workspace for your organization or join an existing one. Your workspace is the shared hub where all strategy work happens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Define Your Strategy">
    Set your top-level strategic goals and break them down into concrete initiatives and measurable milestones. Assign owners, set timelines, and establish the metrics you'll use to track success.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track Progress">
    Monitor execution in real time using the dashboard. Update milestone status, review initiative health, and surface blockers before they derail your plans — keeping every stakeholder aligned as the strategy evolves.
  </Step>
</Steps>
